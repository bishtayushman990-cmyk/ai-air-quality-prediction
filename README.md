# 🌍 AI Air Quality Prediction Platform

> **From monitoring pollution to predicting and preventing it.**

An AI-powered Air Quality Prediction Platform designed to transform urban pollution management from a **reactive approach into a predictive and proactive system**.

The platform combines **historical AQI data, weather data, satellite imagery, and IoT sensor data** with AI/ML techniques to forecast future air quality, detect pollution hotspots, and provide actionable recommendations.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Urban air pollution monitoring is mainly reactive — action is often taken only after pollution levels become dangerous.

### Key Problems

- Existing systems primarily report current AQI with limited predictive capabilities.
- Pollution levels change rapidly due to weather, traffic, industry, and seasonal conditions.
- Citizens need early warnings to take preventive action.
- Authorities need insights into areas likely to experience severe pollution.

### 🎯 Our Goal

**Move from reactive monitoring to predictive air-quality management.**

---
